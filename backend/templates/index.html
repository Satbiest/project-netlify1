How your score is calculated

Skill coverage measures requirements with resume evidence. Semantic similarity compares the meaning of the original resume and job description using MiniLM and cosine similarity. These are guidance indicators, not hiring probabilities.

“Not evidenced” means the skill wasn’t supported by the resume text. You may already have it—consider making that experience clearer.
